Privacy Policy

Fish Hunter Game TDB: Offline · Effective date: September 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Fish Hunter Game TDB: Offline mobile app ("the app", "we", "our") handles information. Please read it together with the privacy policy of Google AdMob, which serves the advertising in the app.

1. Information We Collect

Gameplay data (stored on your device only)

The game itself works offline and does not require an account or sign-in. Game progress is saved locally on your device using on-device storage (Hive):

  • Coins
  • Fish inventory
  • Upgrades
  • Unlocked areas
  • Selected area
  • Daily reward status
  • Music preference

This data never leaves your device and is never sent to us. We do not operate a server and we do not have a copy of your progress.

Data collected by our advertising partner

The app shows advertisements supplied by Google AdMob. To serve, measure and prevent fraud in those ads, Google may collect and process:

  • Advertising ID — a resettable identifier assigned by your device
  • Device and app information — device model, operating system version, app version, language and country settings
  • IP address, from which approximate (city-level) location can be derived
  • Ad interaction data — which ads were shown, viewed or tapped

We do not receive this data in a form that identifies you. We only see aggregate reports, such as how many ads were shown and estimated earnings.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, or precise (GPS) location.

2. How We Use Information

Local gameplay data is used only to save your progress, restore your game state when you reopen the app, and support core gameplay features.

Data collected by Google AdMob is used to display advertising, which is how the app is funded and kept free to play.

3. Advertising

The app contains three kinds of ads:

  • A banner on the menu screens
  • A full-screen ad shown between fishing rounds
  • An optional rewarded video you may choose to watch in exchange for in-game coins — it is never shown unless you tap the button

Depending on your settings and region, ads may be personalised (based on data Google holds about you) or non-personalised.

Your choices:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads, where you can reset your Advertising ID or delete it entirely to opt out of personalised advertising.
  • Users in regions such as the EEA, UK and Switzerland are shown a consent form before personalised advertising is used, and may change that choice at any time.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal data.

Data described in section 1 is shared with Google AdMob for the purpose of serving and measuring advertising. Google's handling of that data is governed by its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Gameplay data remains on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes your locally stored game progress, and it cannot be recovered because we hold no copy of it.

To limit advertising data, reset or delete your Advertising ID using the Android setting described in section 3.

6. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will act on it.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical measures for local data handling within the app. Because gameplay data stays on your device, its security also depends on your device's own protections. No method of storage is completely secure.

8. Internet Access

The game is playable without an internet connection. A connection is used only to request advertisements. If you are offline, the game runs normally and ads simply do not appear.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any update will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
