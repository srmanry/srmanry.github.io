Welcome to Fish Hunter.

Your privacy is important to us, and we are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how the Fish Hunter mobile application handles information and user data.

1. Information We Collect

Fish Hunter does not require account registration or sign-in.

Gameplay data — stored on your device only

The app stores gameplay-related information locally on your device using on-device storage technology (Hive), including:

  • Coins
  • Fish inventory
  • Upgrades
  • Unlocked areas
  • Selected area
  • Daily reward status
  • Music preference

This information never leaves your device and is never sent to us. We operate no server and hold no copy of your progress.

Data collected by our advertising partner

The app displays advertisements supplied by Google AdMob. To serve, measure, and prevent fraud in those ads, Google may collect and process:

  • Advertising ID — a resettable identifier assigned by your device
  • Device and app information — device model, operating system version, app version, language and country settings
  • IP address, from which approximate (city-level) location can be derived
  • Ad interaction data — which ads were shown, viewed, or tapped

We do not receive this information in a form that identifies you. We see only aggregate reports, such as how many ads were shown and estimated earnings.

We do not collect or store personal information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Precise (GPS) location

2. How We Use Information

The locally stored gameplay data is used only to:

  • Save your game progress
  • Restore your game state when reopening the app
  • Support core gameplay functionality and features

Your gameplay data is never used for advertising or user tracking.

Information collected by Google AdMob is used to display advertising, which is how the app is funded and kept free to play.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information.

Gameplay data stays on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

The advertising information described in Section 1 is shared with Google AdMob for the purpose of serving and measuring advertisements. Google's handling of that information is governed by its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy — https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services — https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

4. Ads, Analytics, and Third-Party Services

Fish Hunter uses the Google AdMob advertising SDK. The app shows three kinds of ads:

  • A banner on the menu screens
  • A full-screen ad between fishing rounds
  • An optional rewarded video, shown only when you choose to tap the button, in exchange for in-game coins

Depending on your settings and region, ads may be personalised (based on information Google holds about you) or non-personalised.

Your choices:

  • On Android, open Settings → Google → Ads, where you can reset your Advertising ID or delete it entirely to opt out of personalised advertising.
  • Users in regions such as the EEA, UK, and Switzerland are shown a consent form before personalised advertising is used, and may change that choice at any time.

The app does not run any third-party analytics service.

The app also relies on platform or framework components such as:

  • Flutter runtime
  • Google Play Store distribution services

These services operate under their own privacy policies.

5. Permissions

Fish Hunter requests the following permissions, all of which are used only to display advertisements:

  • Internet access and network state
  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID)

Fish Hunter does not request sensitive device permissions such as:

  • Contacts
  • Camera
  • Microphone
  • Precise location

These permissions are not required for the core gameplay experience.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Your gameplay data remains stored on your device until:

  • You clear the app data, or
  • You uninstall the application

Please note that uninstalling the app may permanently remove locally stored game progress, and it cannot be recovered because we hold no copy of it.

To limit advertising data, reset or delete your Advertising ID using the Android setting described in Section 4.

7. Children's Privacy

Fish Hunter is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will act on it.

8. Security

We apply reasonable technical measures to protect locally stored data within the app. However, no method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

9. Internet Access

The game is playable without an internet connection. A connection is used only to request advertisements. If you are offline, the game runs normally and ads simply do not appear.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

Any future updates will be reflected by revising the Effective Date at the top of this page.
